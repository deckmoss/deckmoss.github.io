## The modern internet is not what you might have expected
- The technology we use today is far different from what anyone expected when it was first introduced.
- The modern internet creates an algorithmic bubble around each visitor.  
- Visitors may perceive the internet through their bubble but might believe that the bubble encompasses the internet itself.

## Predictive Analytics  
Peoples should not trust either big monopolist internet companies nor major phone manufacturers, 
as:  
- They spy constantly on your online and offline activities.  
- They create a very precise profile of your personality.  
- They act as gatekeepers to your algorithmic bubble.

## Micro Targeting  
- Those mighty gatekeepers sell subscriptions that provide access to your personal bubble, allowing individuals and organizations to control information flow within this digital realm.
- Such a subscription targets many people who share those personality traits which the buyer wants to utilize for their purposes.
